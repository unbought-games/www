Give it room.

Leave at least half an O-width around either wordmark. For symbol avatars, use the square 1024 PNG with its built-in 128 px safety margin.

Keep it readable.

  • Symbol: at least 16 px of visible artwork.
  • Wordmark: at least 160 px wide.
  • One-line lockup: at least 260 px wide.
  • Stacked lockup: at least 160 px wide.

Keep Unbought intact. Use the stacked version when horizontal space is tight.

Both words use Space Grotesk 700 at the same type size and weight. Lowercase games shares the baseline; title-case Games sits below, aligned left. Use the supplied artwork rather than retyping it.

Keep the match fair.

Preserve the two equal pieces and their diagonal gap. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor one half, close the gap, add effects, or substitute a normal O. Use a plain, contrasting background.

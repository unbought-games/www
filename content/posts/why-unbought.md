We like browser games. The long plans, the rivalries, the alliances, and the feeling that a decision you made yesterday paid off today.

We don't like watching that competition turn into a question of who spends more.

That's why we're building unbought games. Open-source alternatives that preserve the strategy and remove purchased advantages. Resources, faster production, better information, extra actions: those shouldn't be things you buy your way into.

## Starting with space

[Orbit Imperium is our first game](/news/orbit-imperium-is-live/). It's in open beta, and there's a lot left to build.

We'll run an official version, and the games will also be practical to host yourself. Optional purchases may eventually support the project or provide cosmetics. They won't change your odds of winning.

This news page is where we'll explain what's happening: company news, game updates, and open-source releases. Pick the games you care about, or subscribe to the whole lot through RSS.

We can fix everything. Starting with games.
